Privacy Policy

1. Data Controller

The data controller in the sense of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) is AfroSynthese, Berlin, Germany. Contact: asep.ev.central@gmail.com. Founded in 2020 by Darrell-Fedele Amisi, CEO.

2. Processing of Booking & Media Data

We process client contact details (names, emails, telephone numbers) and event coordinates solely for fulfilling production contracts, issuing invoices, and providing secure client portal downloads. Photographic and video masters are stored on private encrypted cloud vaults.

3. Client Rights under GDPR

You possess the right to access, rectify, or request deletion of your personal data at any time. You may also request exclusion of specific private images from public editorial promotional archives by contacting our executive desk.

4. Cookie Management

We utilize essential session cookies for maintaining user login state and preferences. Non-essential tracking cookies require your explicit consent and can be updated anytime on our Cookie Settings page.
