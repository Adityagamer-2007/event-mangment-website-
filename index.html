THE AFROSYNTHESE MANIFESTO

We Capture More Than Moments. We Capture Meaning.

Founded in Germany in 2020 by Chairman & CEO Darrell-Fedele Amisi, AfroSynthese operates at the pinnacle of international visual culture. We document high-profile concerts, European gala summits, luxury diaspora weddings, and cultural ceremonies with an editorial reverence inspired by the world’s greatest photographic archives.

With dedicated production units active across Western Europe and an experienced creative bureau in Kinshasa, Democratic Republic of Congo, our lens bridges continents with uncompromising aesthetic excellence.
