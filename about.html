OUR ORIGIN & PHILOSOPHY

The Sovereign Lens

An international media house rooted in European precision and Congolese cultural grandeur, dedicated to the highest standards of visual arts.

FOUNDED IN GERMANY, 2020 EUROPE & KINSHASA
A NEW BENCHMARK FOR AFRICAN MEDIA

Bridging Continents Through Visual Mastery

AfroSynthese was founded in Germany in 2020 by Darrell-Fedele Amisi, Chairman and Chief Executive Officer, with a singular and unyielding mandate: to establish a world-class digital media platform that documents pan-African and international milestones with undeniable elegance.

Operating with an executive headquarters in Berlin and an agile, highly experienced field crew in Kinshasa, Democratic Republic of Congo, AfroSynthese delivers high-definition concert coverage, cinematic wedding films, corporate summit reportage, and high-fashion editorial portraiture throughout Western Europe and Central Africa.

Our visual language is uncompromising. Inspired by the legendary editorial archives of world photography, our creators reject generic stock aesthetics in favor of large-format compositions, chiaroscuro lighting, and authentic human dignity.
